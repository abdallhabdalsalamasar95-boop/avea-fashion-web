# Privacy Policy (Template)

**Last updated:** 2026-01-10

This is a starter template for **Carmen Karla**. Replace placeholders, review with a legal professional if needed, and host it publicly (required for Play Store/App Store listings).

## What we collect
- **Account information**: email and a Firebase user ID (UID) when you sign in.
- **App activity**: orders created inside the app, favorites, rewards points and history.
- **Device/diagnostics (optional)**: crash/performance logs if enabled.
- **Advertising identifiers (if ads enabled)**: the ads SDK may collect device identifiers for measurement and ad delivery.

## How we use data
- Provide sign-in, orders, rewards, and notifications.
- Sync your data between sessions/devices when Firebase is enabled.
- Show ads (only if ads are enabled in the build/config).

## Data sharing
- Firebase services (Auth/Firestore/Storage) for backend functionality.
- Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) if ads are enabled.

## Account deletion
You can delete your account from inside the app:
- Open **Profile → Delete Account**.
- The app will delete your Firebase account and will attempt to delete your cloud data.
